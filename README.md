<p><strong>📖 About</strong><br>

<strong>My Nether's Delight Refabricated</strong> is an add-on for <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-refabricated" rel="nofollow"><strong>Farmer's Delight Refabricated</strong></a> focused on adding culinary content to the Nether! Hunt infernal creatures, cultivate invasive crops and use your questionable cooking skills to prepare a myriad of disgusting meals, suspicious desserts and moss-covered snacks.</p>

<p><strong>🍽️ Infernal Cuisine</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hoglins:</strong> turn their loins into sausages, chops, burgers, breakfasts, stews and an enormous Stuffed Hoglin.</li>
<li><strong>Striders:</strong> did you know they are ovoviviparous? Crack their eggs, mince their meat and prepare soups, tarts, stroganoff or a whole Striderloaf.</li>
<li><strong>Ghasts:</strong> there is no flour in the Nether, so a chef has to use whatever is floating around. Apparently, Ghasts taste a little like rice noodles.</li>
<li><strong>Bullet Peppers:</strong> use them in curries, chilidogs, skewers, stuffed peppers and other foods that are probably too spicy for the human body.</li>
<li>Serve placeable feasts such as Stuffed Hoglin, Striderloaf, Ghasta with Cream, Bread Loaves and Magma Cake.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>🔥 The Blazier</strong><br>
The Blazier is a temperamental Nether cooking station that refuses to settle for a single temperature. It has four heat states, ranging from weak embers to a white-hot flame, allowing it to provide the right kind of heat for different cooking processes such as campfire cooking, smoking, baking and smelting.</p>

<p>Its cooking speed can be configured, and the entire block can be disabled if your kitchen already contains enough barely controlled fire hazards.</p>

<p><strong>🌶️ Pungency and Poaching</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Caustic Pungency:</strong> Bullet Peppers and other pungent foods can harm unprotected players near intense heat. Eating extremely spicy food beside an open flame was never a great idea.</li>
<li><strong>Invigorating Pungency:</strong> players protected from fire receive regeneration near heat sources instead. Proper equipment turns a terrible decision into a surprisingly useful one.</li>
<li><strong>Curse of Poaching:</strong> allows swords and axes to obtain certain Farmer's Delight ingredients without using a knife. Just be careful when choosing your prey, or the curse may give you a nasty surprise.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>🌱 Nether Farming</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Powdery Cannon:</strong> bamboo during its worst emo phase, now armed with bright and dangerously spicy berries.</li>
<li><strong>Powdery Cane:</strong> smaller and thinner than the Cannon, but no less invasive. Its base can provide gunpowder and, in the right soil, additional peppers.</li>
<li><strong>Bullet Pepper:</strong> extremely spicy, strangely bright and not advisable to eat beside anything currently on fire.</li>
<li><strong>Leteos Compost:</strong> when the land forgets even its own name, almost any crop can feel at home in it.</li>
<li><strong>Resurgent Soil:</strong> helps crops and plants grow outside their usual environments.</li>
<li><strong>Resurgent Farmland:</strong> water is difficult to find in the Nether, so this farmland uses nearby heat instead.</li>
<li><strong>Fungus Colonies:</strong> Crimson and Warped Fungi can form renewable colonies when planted on Resurgent Soil.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>🧱 Building and Decoration</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Powdery Wood Set:</strong> a complete building set with planks, mosaic blocks, stairs, slabs, fences, doors, trapdoors, signs, hanging signs and more. Bamboo is still going through that emo phase.</li>
<li><strong>Nether Cabinets:</strong> organize your kitchen using Nether Brick, Red Nether Brick, Blackstone Brick and Powdery cabinets.</li>
<li><strong>Nether Stove:</strong> a proper stove for anyone brave enough to build a kitchen in Hell.</li>
<li><strong>Nether Trophies:</strong> display Hoglin, Zoglin, Skoglin and Golden trophies. Remember to wax them unless you want your living room to smell like rotting flesh.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>📝 FAQ</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Why does Pungency have two icons?</strong> The Bullet Pepper icon represents Caustic Pungency, which harms the player near heat. The heart icon represents Invigorating Pungency, granted when the player is properly protected from fire.</li>
<li><strong>Why are my Powdery crops growing so slowly?</strong> They are invasive Nether plants, but their growth still depends on the block beneath them. Resurgent Soil helps them grow faster and produce better yields.</li>
<li><strong>Can I use it in a modpack?</strong> Yes! Please link to the official Modrinth page and do not rehost the files.</li>
